## Mood / feel
<!-- What should this feel like to read? What's the emotional register? -->

Ancient. Heavy. Like a story that has always been true.
The storm should be overwhelming, not atmospheric.
The ending should feel like grief that has gone on so long it became something else.

## Key moments — what matters most
<!-- Moments you feel strongly about. Director will prioritise these. -->

1. The thundercrack / screen black / reboot — this is the hinge of the whole piece.
2. The alates from his body — visceral. Reader should feel it.
3. "A bird, looking for the earth." — the last line. Do not decorate this. Let it land alone.

## What I don't want
<!-- Anything to avoid. -->

- Nothing cute or whimsical. This is not playful weird, it's heavy weird.
- Don't over-animate. Some sections should just be text doing the work.
- The pixel termite effect should feel like loss, not spectacle.

## Platform / constraints
<!-- Mobile? Desktop? Any technical limits? -->

Desktop first for now. Mobile pass later.
