# Essay 1

There is a man that lives in the hotel, she said, that recommended club Cuomo.

He seemed cool, she assures us and I ask if he was wearing wooden beads, like a bhuddist monk might, but she seems confused.

"What made him seem cool?" I ask.

[sigh] "I don't know, he had a cigar," she says.

In the Taxi, I picture cool people, many are black, but more have swords. [angry] None ... have cigars.

I watch the streetlights make little slugs of light crawling over a misty grey night. Could the others in the taxi tell how cool they were? The slugs? None of the slugs had cigars either.

When we get to club Cuomo, [surprised] it turns out pretty cool. Maybe because of all the black people? But I keep my eyes out for swords just in case. 

The night becomes a blur of conversation and I ask the others if they've heard about how you're supposed to put a finger in a dog's rectum when it bites? They seem ignorant of that myth. [tired] I only brought it up to dispell it in the first place. [angry] It doesn't work, trust me. I backtrack and tell them that it doesn't work and I never should have brought it up, since they already knew. [sad] They seem like they agree. 

Our waiter comes, he is middle-aged and skinny. His English is broken. I feel sorry for him, but I cannot say why. He probably never had to have his finger up a pitbull's rectum when it didn't want to release his scared and confused old dog. 

I ask his name, and he says "I'm Brilliant" and I am terrified that my friends will say something about his name, that they will tease him. Because they have a habit of making terrible jokes like that. But they don't. And I am relieved and I am happy. And I think... Wow, Brilliant is a name too. Brilliant is a name too.

We are the last ones in the restaurant. It closes at 1am because of us! [elated] How cool! 

Brilliant leaves at 1:30. He needs to clean first. Minibus taxis are sparse this time of night. He waits another half an hour, then takes two separate taxis to his corrugated tin shack. In the taxi he can't help but marvel at how cool the lights are. They are like little slugs in the mist. He showers in a bucket, by standing in it and squeezing a sponge so that hot water flows from where he sponges back into the bucket. There is no shower in his shack. He is 37 this year. He has been a waiter for 10 of those years. His 10 hour shift starts in 7 hours from now. [sad] Brilliant.

[defeated] Brilliant is a name too. [sad] Brilliant is a person too.

Outwards, there are thousands of Brilliants. There are Gifts and Blessings and Preciouses and one or two Innocents carrying other people's keys. A mother names her child like a prayer, and hands them to the world. 

Those people wear out. The names don't, but the people do. These names are smoothed and handed on, mother to child, prayer to prayer, until they are worn as river stones. 

[slowly] Tough times ever last ... Tough people never do.

Hundreds of Brilliants have painted the name with their blood, soaked it with the darkness of their souls. An offering on the altar of a god. 

[slowly] Brilliant is a god too.
