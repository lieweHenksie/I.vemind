# Essay 1

There is a man that lives in the hotel, she said, that recommended club Cuomo.

He seemed cool, she assures us and I ask if he was wearing wooden beads, like a bhuddist monk might, but she seems confused.

"What made him seem cool?" I ask.

[sigh] "I don't know, he had a cigar," she says.

In the Taxi, I picture cool people, many are black, but more have swords. [angry] None ... have cigars.

I watch the streetlights make little slugs of light crawling over a misty grey night. Could the others in the taxi tell how cool they were? The slugs? None of the slugs had cigars either.

When we get to club Cuomo, [surprised] it turns out pretty cool. Maybe because of all the black people? But I keep my eyes out for swords just in case. 

The night becomes a blur of conversation and I ask the others if they've heard about how you're supposed to put a finger in a dog's rectum when it bites? They seem ignorant of that myth. [tired] I only brought it up to dispell it in the first place. [angry] It doesn't work, trust me. I backtrack and tell them that it doesn't work and I never should have brought it up, since they already knew. [sad] They seem like they agree. 

Our waiter comes, he is middle-aged and skinny. His English is broken. I feel sorry for him, but I cannot say why. He probably never had to have his finger up a pitbull's rectum when it didn't want to release his scared and confused old dog. 

I ask his name, and he says "I'm Brilliant" and I am terrified that my friends will say something about his name, that they will tease him. Because they have a habit of making terrible jokes like that. But they don't. And I am relieved and I am happy. And I think... Wow, Brilliant is a name too. 

[elated] Brilliant is a name too.
