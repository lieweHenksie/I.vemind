I have an ancient goddess floating behind my left ear. 
Her whims are delicate and her lust is powerful.
She comes from colour.

I had no followers to begin with, but the goddess is kind enough to share hers. I wish she wouldn’t.

Her followers are exclusively old and white. They wish they weren’t but they never had the courage to not be. The goddess has decreed that they will die virgins. 

This is their punishment for loving her but not pleasing her. 

The bottle collector, a powerful shaman, weaved the god into my mind one evening in a supernatural phenomena that modern medicine cannot grasp.

The god cannot leave and my head hole cannot heal, this much we know. 
The god resents me slightly, this much we know.

Hating me is something we have in common at least.

It punishes me by pissing wisdom straight into my head hole 
(This is useful for when I need to seem as clever as I truly am) 
and it rewards me by injecting desire into my spine
(This is useful for when I need to seem like I did not receive a homemade lobotomy)

She does the talking. 
Except when I speak to pretty people. 
There I am allowed to embarrass myself.
